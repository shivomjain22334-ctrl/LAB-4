### LAB 4 – Four-Ingredients Build-A-Prompt

In Lab 4, I learned how to create an effective and reliable AI prompt using four important components: **Task, Context, Constraints, and Output Format**. The activity focused on an aviation situation where passengers needed to be informed about a flight delay. The original prompt was incomplete because it did not provide important details such as the flight number, route, delay duration, reason, revised departure time, or communication format.

I then structured the prompt by adding complete operational information. The example used Flight AV102 from Chandigarh to Delhi, delayed by 45 minutes due to adverse weather, with a revised departure time of 4:15 PM. The prompt also instructed the AI to keep the message below 60 words and avoid inventing compensation or policy information.

This activity showed me that providing clear context and constraints reduces assumptions and hallucinations. Specifying the output as an SMS also helped generate a short and suitable passenger notification. Overall, I learned that a well-structured prompt produces more accurate, relevant, and professional AI outputs.
